
Week 5 Assignment: CRM Usage in ITT (IntimeTec) 

1. How can ITT (IntimeTec), our software company, use CRM? 

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) helps ITT manage interactions with customers, prospects, and business partners 
in a centralized system. As a software company, ITT can use CRM to track leads from marketing campaigns, manage the sales pipeline, 
record customer requirements, and monitor project opportunities. CRM can also store customer contact information, communication history, 
contracts, support requests, and feedback. Different teams such as Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Support can access the 
same customer data, improving collaboration and ensuring better customer service. In addition, CRM can automate follow-ups, 
reminders, email campaigns, and reporting activities, helping employees work more efficiently and maintain strong relationships with clients. 

2. How can using CRM in ITT (IntimeTec) make an impact? 

Implementing CRM can have a significant positive impact on ITT. It can increase sales productivity by helping teams 
identify and prioritize potential customers. CRM improves customer satisfaction because employees can quickly access 
customer information and respond faster to requests or issues. It provides better visibility into business opportunities, 
enabling management to make data-driven decisions. CRM also improves team collaboration by keeping all customer-related 
information in one place. Through automation and analytics, ITT can reduce manual work, improve efficiency, 
increase customer retention, and generate higher revenue. Overall, CRM helps ITT build stronger customer relationships 
and achieve sustainable business growth.
