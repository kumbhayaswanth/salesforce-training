

Week 6 Assignment: ISP Management App in Salesforce CRM 

1) Relationships Created and Reasons 

• Customer -> Service Connection (One-to-Many): A customer can have multiple internet connections. 
• Subscription Plan -> Service Connection (One-to-Many): Many customers can subscribe to the same plan. 
• Customer -> Billing (One-to-Many): A customer receives multiple bills over time. 
• Customer -> Support Ticket (One-to-Many): A customer can raise multiple support requests. 
• Service Connection -> Support Ticket (Lookup Relationship): Tickets are linked to a specific connection for faster troubleshooting. 
 
These relationships reduce data duplication, improve reporting, and provide a complete view of customer activities. 


2) Use of Standard Objects 

User: Represents employees such as sales representatives, support engineers, and administrators. 
Lead: Stores potential customers who have shown interest in ISP services before becoming customers. 
Account: Represents a customer organization or individual customer after qualification. 
Contact: Stores customer contact details such as name, phone number, and email address. 
Opportunity: Tracks potential sales deals for new internet connections, upgrades, or renewals. 
Product: Represents ISP plans and services such as Broadband, Fiber Internet, and Business Plans. 
Case: Manages customer support issues, complaints, service outages, and technical problems.
